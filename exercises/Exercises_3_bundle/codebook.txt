Exercises 3 — Data codebook

All datasets are fictional and were generated for classroom use. A row represents one independent unit except where measurements are paired within the same row. All missing values have been excluded.

case_processing.csv (n = 45)
case_id: unique case identifier
processing_days: time in days to process a case (numeric)
The historical benchmark is 20 days.

program_outcomes.csv (n = 84)
participant_id: unique participant identifier
program: Traditional or Diversion (two independent groups)
days_to_resolution: elapsed days until case resolution (numeric)
Assume each participant appears once and was sampled independently.

training_scores.csv (n = 36)
officer_id: unique officer identifier
score_before: assessment score before training (numeric)
score_after: assessment score after training (numeric)
The same officer took both assessments. Higher scores indicate better performance.

service_satisfaction.csv (n = 96)
response_id: unique respondent identifier
contact_method: Email, Phone, or InPerson (three independent groups)
satisfaction_score: respondent rating on a 1–10 scale (treated as numeric for this practice exercise)
Assume independent respondents. Note the scale is bounded; check plots and discuss whether a classical ANOVA approximation is reasonable.

survey_responses.csv (n = 120)
respondent_id: unique respondent identifier
residence: Urban or Suburban
supports_policy: Yes or No
Each respondent answered once. Assume independent survey responses.
